    Research targets

    • An upper bound for the excess T, or the exclusion of any T above 6. Status
    • An upper bound for the special exponent a, or the inequality qa < m. Status
    • An upper bound for v3(N) in the restrained family; lower bounds alone cannot close it. Status
    • s exceptions to a common index prime l with 16s ≥ l (smaller s is settled; also three exceptions for l ≥ 359 and two for l ≥ 23); common index families with rows that are not prime powers (at least four such rows for every odd prime l, and at least K once 16K < l; also l ≤ 16t24t for the number t of prime factors that are 1 modulo l; the family is open). Status
