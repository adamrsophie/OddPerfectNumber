Source
Open problem. Whether an odd perfect number exists is unknown. This project does not claim a proof; it formalizes necessary conditions.

Survey register

The 287 arXiv papers screened for the survey, with the grade each received from its title and abstract.

The grade measures relevance to this project and says nothing about the quality or correctness of a paper. A (56 papers): directly about odd perfect numbers. B (75 papers): a technique that transfers (divisor sums, abundancy, cyclotomic values, primitive prime divisors, related equations). C (156 papers): matched the search terms but unrelated. The 131 papers graded A or B were read in full.

The search queries ran on arXiv only, so journal papers without an arXiv version are missing from this list; several of them are cited in the module descriptions. The one line reasons, the reading notes, the list of reviewed repositories and the readings of papers that claim a proof are not published.
